Lodging

Resort

The island's 4-star resort offers a full-service luxury experience with oceanfront views, a private beach, fine dining, and guided excursions. Guests can enjoy spacious rooms, a relaxing spa, and a poolside atmosphere designed for comfort and convenience.


Hotels

Taniti's small, family-owned hotels provide a more personal and authentic stay. These charming properties often feature locally inspired décor, friendly service, and easy access to nearby shops, restaurants, and beaches.


Hostels and B&B

For budget-conscious travelers, Taniti's growing selection of hostels and bed and breakfasts offers affordable and welcoming accommodations. These options are perfect for meeting fellow visitors, enjoying home-cooked meals, and experiencing the island in a relaxed, community-oriented setting.
